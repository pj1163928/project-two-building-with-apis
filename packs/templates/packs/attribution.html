How to credit

A good credit names four things: the title, the author, the source (where you found it) and the license, linked when you can. Say if you changed it.

"Footsteps, Stones, A.wav" by InspectorJ (freesound.org), CC BY 4.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ · trimmed and layered
  • Where: the game's credits screen, its README or a CREDITS.txt beside it, and the store page. Anywhere a player could reasonably find it.
  • The easy way: each pack's attribution page has a ready Attribution and License Notice to copy, and Download files puts the same text in CREDITS.txt. The manifest carries the credits too, in credits.
  • Openverse's own line: Openverse writes an attribution for each sound, and the Toolbox uses it as it is.

Optional credits

CC0 assets owe nothing, so these are up to you. A thank-you helps the people and sites that make free assets possible, and players like to know.

Textures and HDRIs from Poly Haven (polyhaven.com) and ambientCG (ambientcg.com), CC0.
